# Bitvoya MCP v0.3.3

Adds a fast price signal for shortlisted hotels.

Hosted endpoint:

- `https://bitvoya.com/api/mcp`

## New tool: `get_hotel_prices`

- Input: `hotel_ids` (1 to 20 ids from a search result), `checkin`, `checkout`, optional `adult_num`.
- Output, per hotel: `available`, `from_per_night_cny` (the supplier's lowest average nightly price in CNY, before the Bitvoya service fee), and the supplier currency and amount.
- Requires only `inventory.read`.

Search results keep a short time budget for prices (1.6 s), so in large cities the shortlist often comes back without them. `get_hotel_prices` waits up to 12 s for just the hotels on the shortlist; a cold lookup for five Paris hotels takes about 4 s, a warm one under 100 ms.

`get_hotel_rooms` is still the source of the payable total (`display_total_cny`).

See [v0.3.2](v0.3.2.md) for the previous release.
